# Retain archived data until explicit deletion

Longitudinal analysis benefits from continuity more than the integration
benefits from bounded retention, and projected five-year growth is acceptable.
Recorder records, Store documents, summaries, and FIT files therefore have no
automatic expiry; disablement, upgrade, and rollback preserve them, while any
future deletion remains a separate explicit operator action. The beta exposes
no archive-deletion action or bulk-clear control.
